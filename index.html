CalSync

Keeps you showing as busy on all your Google calendars.

For every real event on one of your calendars, CalSync writes a private "Busy" block to your other calendars and keeps it current. Only the time is shared: titles, attendees, and descriptions are never even requested from Google.

It's for people who juggle several Google accounts (their own company, clients, a school, a personal Gmail) and want each one to show when they're actually free. CalSync runs on the user's own always-on computer, such as a Raspberry Pi. There is no CalSync cloud service.

Privacy policy

CalSync is software that each person runs on their own computer, with their own Google Cloud project. There is no CalSync service: its authors never receive, see, or store your data.

What CalSync reads from Google

For each connected calendar, CalSync asks Google only for these fields of each event:

  • its id and, for recurring events, the series id
  • its start and end time
  • whether it's cancelled, and whether it's marked busy or free
  • its event type (for example, out of office)
  • your own answer to an invite (accepted, declined, maybe, or unanswered), and whether you're the organizer
  • CalSync's own private marker, which identifies the Busy blocks it created

It never requests titles, descriptions, locations, attachments, conference links, or other attendees' names or addresses. It also reads your account's email address once, at sign-in, to confirm you signed in to the right account.

What CalSync writes to Google

CalSync creates, updates, and deletes private events titled "Busy" on your connected calendars, one for each real event elsewhere. Each carries only a time and a hidden marker: a short account id (such as c01) and the original event's id. It never changes or deletes any event it didn't create.

What stays on your computer

  • The sign-in tokens for each account, readable only by your user.
  • Your config file, with account labels and email addresses.
  • A small status file with the time and result of the last sync.
  • Logs, which show account ids, event ids, and times, never titles.

None of this is sent anywhere except to Google's Calendar API. If a health-check address is configured, CalSync also sends an empty ping to it after each successful sync. The ping contains no calendar data.

Your control

You can stop CalSync at any time by turning off its schedule, and remove its access to any account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. CalSync's offboard command removes every Busy block it created from an account.

CalSync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
